1. Purpose of the Paywall
The paywall exists to:
protect pharmacy labor from unpaid work
prevent patients from bypassing the platform once a pharmacy confirms inventory
enforce structured, compliant communication before identities are revealed
ensure the platform only earns when real value (confirmed inventory + connection) is delivered
No pharmacy or patient identity is exposed until the paywall is completed.
2. Pre‑Paywall Rules (Locked State)
Before the patient pays:
Pharmacy identity is hidden
no name
no address
no phone
no branding
Patient identity is hidden
no name
no contact info
no insurance details
Communication is restricted to structured prompts only
pharmacies can refine drug identity (manufacturer, strength, form, quantity)
no free‑text messaging
no pickup instructions
no “call us” or “come here” messages
No direct contact channels
no phone calls
no direct chat
no directions
The pre‑paywall state is purely clarification and decision, not relationship.
3. Paywall Trigger
The paywall is triggered when:
A pharmacy accepts a patient request (with or without prior clarification).
The system confirms that inventory is available for the requested product.
The patient is notified:
“A pharmacy has confirmed they have your medication.
To see who they are and connect, complete payment.”
At this point, the patient must decide whether to proceed.
4. What the Patient Pays For
The patient is paying for:
confirmation that a real pharmacy has the requested medication in stock
the identity of that pharmacy (name, address, phone)
a direct connection channel (chat/call/directions)
the work the pharmacy did to clarify and confirm inventory
The payment is not for:
clinical advice
prescription transfer
dispensing
pickup readiness
It is strictly for inventory confirmation + connection.
5. Post‑Paywall Rules (Unlocked State)
After the patient pays:
Pharmacy identity is revealed
name
address
phone
map/directions
Patient identity is revealed to the pharmacy
name
contact info (as allowed by app design)
Direct communication is enabled
chat or messaging
phone call
pickup instructions
transfer discussions (if allowed by regulation and design)
The relationship moves from platform‑mediated to direct pharmacy–patient.
6. What Cannot Happen Before Paywall
To protect the paywall and prevent leakage:
pharmacies cannot send any identifying information in clarifications
patients cannot request the pharmacy’s name or contact info
the app cannot show “nearby pharmacy has it, call them here”
no screenshots or hints of pharmacy identity are allowed
no “workaround” channels (e.g., external links) are permitted
Any attempt to bypass the paywall is a violation and grounds for removal.
7. Paywall Failure Scenarios
If the patient does not complete payment:
pharmacy identity remains hidden
no direct communication is opened
the request may expire or be re‑routed
no reimbursement is issued to the pharmacy
If payment fails (technical or financial):
the system retries or prompts the patient
if unresolved, the request returns to a pre‑paywall state
pharmacy is not penalized, but not reimbursed
8. Interaction with Clarification and Reimbursement
Clarification happens before paywall.
Acceptance triggers paywall offer to the patient.
Payment completion triggers:
identity unlock
connection
reimbursement event (if clarification led to acceptance).
The paywall is the gate between “pharmacy did work” and “pharmacy gets paid”.
9. Interaction with Move‑List
Move‑list is internal and does not affect paywall rules.
A move‑list match may increase the chance a pharmacy accepts a request.
Even for move‑list items, identity is locked until paywall completion.
No special bypass or discount is applied purely because a drug is on the move‑list.
10. Summary
The paywall:
locks identity and direct communication until value is created
protects pharmacy labor
keeps communication structured and compliant
ensures the platform earns only when it delivers confirmed inventory + connection
ties directly into clarification and reimbursement logic
It is the central gate that turns queue activity into real, compensated pharmacy–patient connections.
