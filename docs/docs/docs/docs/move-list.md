Move‑List Document
1. Purpose of the Move‑List
The move‑list allows pharmacies to privately mark certain drug products they want to dispense more quickly.
Typical reasons include:
slow‑moving inventory
high‑cost items
short‑dated stock
excess quantity on hand
manufacturer discontinuations
strategic inventory rotation
The move‑list is internal only and is never visible to patients.
2. What the Move‑List Is
The move‑list is a simple, private list of drug products maintained by each pharmacy.
It is used to:
highlight incoming patient requests that match listed items
increase visibility of desirable requests
help pharmacies move inventory without exposing stock levels
The move‑list does not function as an inventory system.
3. What the Move‑List Is Not
The move‑list does not:
show inventory to patients
guarantee availability
expose stock levels
replace the queue
affect reimbursement amounts
bypass the paywall
reveal pharmacy identity
It is strictly an internal prioritization tool.
4. How the Move‑List Works
Pharmacy adds a drug product to its move‑list.
The backend stores the item privately.
When a patient request matches the item:
the pharmacy receives a priority notification
the request is visually highlighted in the queue
Pharmacy may choose to clarify or accept the request.
Normal paywall and reimbursement rules apply.
The move‑list only increases visibility, not compensation.
5. Adding Items to the Move‑List
Pharmacies may add items using:
NDC
drug name
strength
dosage form
manufacturer
Each entry must match the same drug identity fields used in the queue.
Pharmacies may remove or update items at any time.
6. Priority Notification Logic
When a patient request matches a move‑list item:
the pharmacy receives a priority alert
the request is flagged in the queue
the pharmacy can respond faster
no patient sees or knows this occurred
Priority notifications help pharmacies dispense inventory they prefer to move.
7. Compliance & Privacy Rules
To protect privacy and prevent misuse:
move‑list items are never shown to patients
move‑list items do not imply inventory availability
move‑list items do not unlock identity
move‑list items do not bypass the paywall
move‑list items do not increase reimbursement
The move‑list is strictly internal and compliant.
8. Interaction With Clarification Workflow
If a move‑list item matches a patient request:
the pharmacy may clarify normally
the pharmacy may accept normally
the patient still must complete the paywall
reimbursement rules remain unchanged
Move‑list does not alter the clarification engine.
9. Interaction With Inventory
The move‑list does not require:
inventory APIs
stock uploads
automated inventory feeds
Pharmacies manually verify inventory before accepting a request.
The move‑list is not an inventory declaration.
10. Summary
The move‑list is a private, internal tool that:
increases visibility of desirable requests
helps pharmacies move specific inventory
never exposes stock levels
never reveals identity
never affects reimbursement
never bypasses the paywall
It is a simple, safe, effective prioritization mechanism within the pharmacy app.
