Pharmacy Reimbursement Policy
1. Purpose
This policy ensures pharmacies are compensated when they provide meaningful clarification that results in a successful acceptance of a patient request. Reimbursement protects pharmacist labor, aligns incentives, and supports accurate medication matching without requiring inventory APIs.
2. Definition of a Clarification
A clarification is a structured response from the pharmacy that refines the drug product identity. Valid clarifications may include:
Manufacturer
Strength
Dosage form
Quantity
Clarifications must use the app’s structured prompt system.
Clarifications may not include:
Pharmacy name
Phone number
Address
Pickup instructions
Staff identity
Any direct communication
These restrictions prevent bypassing the paywall and ensure compliance.
3. Conditions Required to Earn Reimbursement
A pharmacy earns reimbursement only when all of the following occur:
The pharmacy submits a clarification.
The patient accepts the clarification.
The pharmacy accepts the request.
The patient completes the paywall.
Pharmacy and patient identities unlock.
A connection is successfully established.
If any step fails, reimbursement is not issued.
4. Reimbursement Amount (Undetermined)
The reimbursement amount is not yet finalized.
A flat‑fee model will be used, but the exact value will be determined during pilot testing based on:
Average pharmacist time per clarification
Clarification acceptance rate
Patient paywall completion rate
Geographic labor differences
Platform sustainability
Pharmacies will receive advance notice before reimbursement amounts are activated.
5. Non‑Qualifying Events
Reimbursement is not issued for:
Clarifications that do not lead to acceptance
Acceptances without clarification
Incorrect clarifications
Patient abandonment before paywall
Pharmacy rejection
Inventory mismatch after acceptance
Only successful clarification‑led acceptances qualify.
6. Anti‑Gaming Rules
To maintain integrity:
No identity exchange before paywall completion
Clarifications must be accurate and match real inventory
Repeated incorrect clarifications reduce routing priority
Queue filtering replaces inventory APIs; pharmacies must verify stock manually
Attempts to bypass the paywall result in removal from the network
7. Reimbursement Trigger Workflow
Reimbursement is triggered automatically when:
Clarification submitted
Clarification accepted
Pharmacy acceptance
Paywall completion
Identity unlock
Connection established
Each event is logged and stored in the pharmacy’s reimbursement ledger.
8. Move‑List Interaction
Pharmacies may maintain an internal move‑list of products they wish to dispense.
This list:
Is not visible to consumers
Does not expose inventory
Generates priority notifications when matched
Does not increase reimbursement amounts
Move‑list functionality improves visibility, not compensation.
9. Payment Schedule
Pharmacies are paid:
Weekly or bi‑weekly
Via ACH or platform credit
With a detailed ledger of all clarification‑led acceptances
Each ledger entry includes:
Request ID
Drug product
Clarification timestamp
Acceptance timestamp
Paywall completion timestamp
Reimbursement status
10. Summary
Pharmacies are reimbursed only when their clarification leads to a completed acceptance.
This ensures:
Protected pharmacist labor
Clean incentives
High‑quality clarifications
Sustainable platform economics
Strong pharmacy engagement
